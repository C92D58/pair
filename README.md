# PAIR

找一對，連一條線。清空棋盤，進入下一關。

A quiet pair-matching game — geometric tiles, ≤2-turn connections, nineteen progressively larger boards up to 12×12 (72 pairs).

Every level is rated out of three stars based on your time, and your total stars earn a rank: 初学 → 见习 → 熟练 → 巧手 → 大师 → 宗师.

Play online: **https://pair.wahsun.org/**
